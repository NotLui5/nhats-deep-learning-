What is the sample-person file about?
Sample Person (SP) file is the core dataset containing detailed, individual-level information gathered directly from the primary survey participants

What is the other-person file about?
The Other Person (OP) file is a specialized, person-level dataset structured around a cumulative roster of individuals connected to the primary survey participant 

What is the tracker file about?
The Tracker file is a public-use data component designed to track the status of the entire selected sample across every round of the longitudinal study

Why might these files contain different numbers of records or represent different units of observation?
In the National Health and Aging Trends Study (NHATS) database, different data files vary in record counts and represent different units of observation because the study captures multiple relational layers of data around an aging sample population
Different Units of Observation - Discrepancies in Record Counts - Sub-study or Supplemental Sampling
